# CJIE Pay

**Demo — fake XRP only. No real money.**

CJIE Pay lets ordinary workers get paid in XRP. Painters, trailer workers,
cleaners, kitchen workers, delivery workers — people who get paid in cash
and want XRP instead.

## How it works

Pick a job. See the price in dollars and in XRP. Tap Pay. The pile grows.

The worker never buys anything — they earn the XRP. The payer is the one
who needs XRP already. Bosses who pay cash stay on the log first: the cash
is logged, then turned into XRP by the worker.

## The demo

Open `index.html` in a browser. Pick a job, tap Pay — Demo, and watch the
pile grow from 136 XRP. The practice price is $1.17 per XRP.

## The plan

1. Real app that saves — every worker has their own log that stays.
2. Easy wallets — simple setup in plain words, no crypto knowledge needed.
3. First 50 workers in Toronto getting paid in XRP.
4. More cities — Toronto first, then Regina, then across Canada.
5. Boss side — buy XRP once, pay many workers.
6. Teaching — plain-words guides and videos so workers learn it fast.
7. Family sending — send XRP to family, fast and cheap, no bank needed to receive, just a wallet.

gets paid $70–$100 cash a day, and logs each day as XRP. He is learning
